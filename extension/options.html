easy-read: reading aloud

Voice and subtitles for “Read aloud from here” (right-click), the 🔊 button and Alt+Shift+R. The look of the subtitles comes from your easy-read settings (the “Aa” panel on any page).

Scanned PDFs (Gemini)

Questions about the paper (Claude)

A question sends the whole text of the paper to Anthropic. The approximate price is shown under each answer.

Translation and PDF clean-up (DeepSeek)

A translation sends the selected text to this service. “Clean up with DeepSeek” in the PDF viewer sends the text of the PDF.
